Key Contributions and Results

Contributions

Proposed an end-to-end hierarchy-aware global model for HTC

Introduced bidirectional TreeLSTM and hierarchy-GCN as structure encoders

Extended framework into parallel (multi-label attention) and serial (text propagation) variants

Empirical Achievements

Achieved significant improvements on three benchmark datasets (RCV1-V2, WOS, NYT)

Particularly effective for low-frequency labels deep in the hierarchy

Outperformed state-of-the-art on RCV1-V2 dataset with best model achieving Macro-F1 63.35%
