Modeling Label Dependencies

Prior Hierarchy Information

Taxonomic hierarchy describes hierarchical relations among labels

Major bottleneck: how to effectively utilize this established structure

Previous methods: static pipeline, hierarchical model, label assignment

HiAGM leverages prior knowledge of label correlations from hierarchy and corpus

Edge $e_{i,j}$ between parent $v_i$ and child $v_j$ carries prior probability

Edge feature $f(e_{i,j})$ represented by $P(U_j|U_i)$ and $P(U_i|U_j)$

Normalization ensures child node probabilities sum to 1

Eq. (1) — Label Conditional Probabilities
$$P(U_j \mid U_i) = \frac{P(U_j \cap U_i)}{P(U_i)} = \frac{P(U_j)}{P(U_i)} = \frac{N_j}{N_i}$$ $$P(U_i \mid U_j) = \frac{P(U_i \cap U_j)}{P(U_j)} = \frac{P(U_j)}{P(U_j)} = 1.0$$
$N_j$: training samples with label $U_j$;  $N_i$: training samples with label $U_i$.
Top-down: $P(U_j|U_i)=N_j/N_i$;  Bottom-up: always 1.0.
