Variant Comparison

Both HiAGM-LA and HiAGM-TP consistently outperform all global text classification baselines (TextRNN / TextCNN / TextRCNN) across four benchmark datasets.
Enhanced text features are beneficial — HiAGM-TP achieves higher Macro-F1 scores than HiAGM-LA in both Hierarchy-GCN and BiTreeLSTM structure encoders.
HiAGM-TP (GCN) records the best Macro-F1 on NYT (60.83) and WOS (80.28), confirming the advantage of direct text-to-hierarchy feature propagation.
