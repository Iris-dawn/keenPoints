Architecture Overview

HiAGM leverages both hierarchy structure and corpus statistics for label modeling

Two variants: HiAGM-LA (inductive) and HiAGM-TP (deductive)

Structure encoder models label dependencies in taxonomic hierarchy

Uses prior probabilities of label co-occurrence for edge weights
